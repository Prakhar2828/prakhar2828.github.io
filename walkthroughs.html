01 / MISSION OPERATIONS / C++17 + WEBASSEMBLY

Asteria
Command. Acknowledge. Protect.

A flight/ground software lab that makes command outcomes, autonomous protection and operational evidence visible.

Try Asteria ↗Inspect the source ↗

Independent software lab with synthetic telemetry. No flight qualification or network/orbital latency claim.

Read the full walkthrough & engineering decisions

  1. Commission the spacecraft

    I separate spacecraft state from ground intent. BOOT, POINT and PAYLOAD_ON establish a nominal operating sequence; the power and payload channels then respond to the running C++ model.

    Inspect captured state 1 ↗

  2. Make success an acknowledged outcome

    The procedure advances on acknowledgements. The audit retains the requested command and its terminal outcome, so an operator can inspect what the flight process accepted. The captured audit also records the later fault injection.

    Inspect captured state 2 ↗

  3. Let onboard protection act

    I inject battery undervoltage and advance the clock. The authoritative state machine enters SAFE, sheds payload load and holds recovery behind battery, fault-clearance and healthy-dwell gates. A button click cannot bypass those conditions.

    Inspect captured state 3 ↗

  4. Reconstruct instead of guessing

    The recorded journal supports a telemetry replay and a generated debrief: seed 42, seven received records, four acknowledged commands and a last received SAFE mode. Replay reads evidence without reissuing commands.

    Inspect captured state 4 ↗

02 / MISSION ANALYSIS / PUBLIC EVIDENCE + PHYSICS

A2 MissionLab
One mission. Traceable reasoning.

A public-data mission reconstruction connecting trajectory geometry, engineering models and the precision of the available evidence.

Try A2 MissionLab ↗Inspect the source ↗

Independent public-data reconstruction. Models are educational approximations; no private Orion telemetry or targeting solution is represented.

Read the full walkthrough & engineering decisions

  1. Align the mission clock

    I put public trajectory products and events on a shared mission clock. At pericynthion, the explorer shows the refined 8,281.938 km Moon-center distance and labels the result as derived from public trajectory data.

    Inspect captured state 1 ↗

  2. Propagate a decision

    I change the RTC2 magnitude from the reported 5.3 ft/s to a modeled 5.4 ft/s. The two-body response changes to about 296 km separation at the reference entry epoch. Direction and propagated consequences stay explicitly modeled.

    Inspect captured state 2 ↗

  3. Keep geometry and heating distinct

    At analysis +85 s, the entry experience connects a reconstructed altitude/speed profile to bounded atmosphere, Mach and heating models. Derived flight geometry and modeled external heating keep separate evidence labels.

    Inspect captured state 3 ↗

  4. Expose the evidence policy

    The technical report carries methods, source provenance and the reported/derived/modeled/inferred/unknown taxonomy. I make the boundary inspectable so an attractive visualization does not imply knowledge the public record lacks.

03 / RESEARCH ENGINEERING / NUMERICS + REPRODUCIBILITY

Wind VISION
Faster. Compared. Reproducible.

A numerical optimization study paired with a held-out reconstruction comparison that gives the simpler baseline its due.

Try Wind VISION ↗Inspect the source ↗Download the replay release ↗

Synthetic, single-component, fixed-geometry spatial reconstruction. CUDA timings are historical; CPU replay rescores frozen neural predictions and regenerates classical baselines.

Read the full walkthrough & engineering decisions

  1. Separate framework from optimization

    I compare four arms: original and vectorized target computation in PyTorch and PhysicsNeMo. The raw timing medians attribute the 5.55× A/D ratio to frequency vectorization, rather than the framework name.

    Inspect captured state 1 ↗

  2. Respect the stronger baseline

    The observation-only estimated-mean + IDW method wins at 0.681607 m/s RMSE over 872 held-out fields. I report that result instead of treating a neural prior as automatically superior.

    Inspect captured state 2 ↗

  3. Inspect what RMSE can hide

    Original unconditional-generation figures expose missing high-frequency power and oversmoothing. Those generation diagnostics are a separate experiment from the held-out reconstruction score.

    Inspect captured state 3 ↗

  4. Let someone else check the numbers

    The public release supplies test data, frozen predictions, a locked protocol and pinned CPU dependencies. A fresh extraction matches all eight methods, regenerates three baselines and reproduces seven bootstrap intervals. GPU training is outside that verified scope.

    Inspect captured state 4 ↗
